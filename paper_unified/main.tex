\begin{abstract}
A calcium ion strengthens the synapse that admitted it. A trader's buying moves the price her belief was about. A language model conditions on its own output. An AI agent pays for data from its own wallet. Different substrates, one shape: a system acting on a world containing itself, rewriting the conditions for its next actions. We synthesize evidence from neuroscience, quantitative finance, artificial intelligence, contemplative traditions, and cosmology around a single invariant: stable reflexive loops develop five organs --- boundary, two-speed memory, salience gate, damping, internal observer --- or are exploited by the task distribution that needs them. Three independent 2026 results triangulate the witness thesis (Panickssery $r=0.94$; Weco AIDE\textsuperscript{2} 63\%$\to$34\%; Dream-RSI 42\% fewer calls); recursive self-improvement sits honestly at Level~1 of 4 with the evaluator as binding constraint; evolved wiring transfers to machines while human scaling stays map-blocked; machine economies acquire real stakes through self-funding. Each claim carries an evidence grade (measured, theoretical, convergent, analogical, open). The practical conclusion is unchanged and now industrially urgent: checkability, not capability, is the scarce resource, and witnesses must precede power. \\[4pt]
\textit{Keywords:} reflexivity, Goodhart's law, global workspace, predictive processing, AI alignment, recursive self-improvement, connectomics, agent economies, provenance, metacognition.

\section{Introduction: One Loop, Five Organs}
\label{sec:intro}
%==============================================================================

\begin{definition}[Reflexive loop]
A dynamical system $S$ coupled to a world $W$ is \emph{reflexive} iff $S \subset W$ in the causally relevant sense: actions of $S$ alter $W$, and the altered $W$ constitutes the input to $S$'s next act. Formally, with state $x_t$ and observation $y_t = h(x_t, a_{<t})$ depending on the system's own action history, the map is part of the territory.
\end{definition}

For a new reader: think of a loop where the output folds back into the input. A credit rating predicts default risk, the published rating then changes borrowing cost, which changes default risk itself. A brain predicts what it will feel, that prediction triggers body responses, which become the sensations the brain then perceives. A model writes a summary, the next prompt includes that summary as context, so the model reads its own prior claim as if it were data. In each case the system cannot step outside to get a clean view. Its beliefs help make the world its beliefs are about. That is reflexivity. It is not a metaphor. It is a causal topology where the arrow from world to system passes through the system's own prior acts.

Two channels carry every action outward: a \textbf{physical channel} (the order executes, calcium floods, tokens are spent) and an \textbf{informational channel} (others observe the act and change behavior because of what it \emph{meant}). When both run, three consequences break normal logic at every scale: models rot from use, measurement corrupts, truth moves (\S\ref{sec:markets}).

\begin{proposition}[The five organs]
Any reflexive loop that persists under selection pressure develops, or is exploited for lacking, five functional organs: (1) \textbf{boundary} --- what counts as inside vs outside; (2) \textbf{two-speed memory} --- fast labile plus slow structural storage, with a consolidation operation; (3) \textbf{salience gate} --- what gets tagged for consolidation; (4) \textbf{damping} --- what prevents runaway positive feedback; (5) \textbf{internal observer} --- a slower loop watching a faster one, with veto power, measured as $\gamma$ = changed/diagnosed.
\end{proposition}

For the new reader, what each organ does and why it cannot be skipped:

\textbf{Boundary} decides what counts as the system and what counts as world. Without it attribution fails. A loss on a subsidiary with no legal link to the parent is not your loss until a gate reclassifies it. Mechanism: membership tests that draw the line. Break it and risk walks across the line. Lose the boundary and you lose the ability to say whose loss this is.

\textbf{Two-speed memory} keeps a fast working store that can learn in one shot and a slow structural store that learns across many examples, plus a consolidation operation that moves tagged traces from fast to slow. Mechanism: hippocampal style fast trace plus cortical slow weights, or working context plus nightly replay and checkpoint. Break it and the system either forgets by morning or freezes on first impression. It needs both speeds.

\textbf{Salience gate} decides what is tagged for consolidation. Not everything deserves to persist. Mechanism: surprise, affect, or error magnitude opens the gate, the mundane does not. Break it and trivia crowds out signal, or important rare events never get written. The gate sets what the future will remember.

\textbf{Damping} prevents positive feedback from running to the wall. Mechanism: inhibition, normalization, downscaling, circuit breakers, cool downs. Without it a detection becomes a panic, a price move becomes a crash, a repetition becomes a fixed loop. Damping bounds the gain.

\textbf{Internal observer} is a slower loop watching a faster one with veto power. Mechanism: mismatch monitoring, drift detection, private scoring, adversarial re checking. Its yield is $\gamma$ equals changed over diagnosed. If $\gamma$ is near zero the watcher reports but never steers. Lose it and the loop cannot correct its own bias because no separate vantage exists.

\textit{Grade: analogical (interpretive).} The boundary organ has a precise image in the Barzakh tradition (Qur'an 55:19-20: two seas meet with a \emph{barzakh} between them, neither transgressing). Read as design, not doctrine: a barzakh is an isthmus that is both and neither, with properties of both sides. It recurs at five scales: skin (inside vs world), now (200-500\,ms stitched present), dream (offline simulation), heart (value integration), cosmos (interface of seen and unseen). At each scale the fence does not merely divide, it \emph{constitutes} the relation by gating what crosses. Its faculty is \emph{khayal} (imagination): counterfactual simulation, the mind's offline world where alternatives are tried without paying the price. Its completed form is the Perfect Man (\emph{al-insan al-kamil}): not a saint only, but the loop fully harnessed, where the observer watches, the gate holds, and the broadcast serves the whole. Ibn Arabi's seven doors of the heart map onto the five organs by collapsing openings that are gates by another name. Five remains the minimal count. This reading is interpretive. It guides architecture. It does not count as measured evidence.

A second image clarifies what a loop can and cannot guarantee. Ash'arite occasionalism denies necessary causation. What we call cause is habit (\emph{ada}): cotton is followed by burning when fire touches it, given the habitual course (\emph{sunna}) under condition $C$, not produced by fire itself. In finance this is \emph{riba} versus \emph{musharaka}. Riba writes a fixed increment regardless of outcome, a claim that pretends necessity. Musharaka shares realized outcome, its return is followed by profit or loss as it occurs. Ledgers and honesty stand in the same relation. A verifiable ledger is followed by honesty under a witness that checks it. No necessary link. Only \emph{is-followed-by} given $S$ under $C$ as \emph{ada}. Remove the witness and honesty does not follow. Keep it and honesty follows by habit, not by guarantee.

\section{Markets: Where Belief Becomes Price}
\label{sec:markets}
%==============================================================================

\textit{Grade: theoretical (Soros, Goodhart) + measured (GOOGL readings, Sept 2026).}

\subsection{Reflexivity: the map becomes the territory}

Standard economics held that prices reflect fundamentals the way a thermometer reflects temperature. Soros \citep{soros} argued the thermometer heats the room: investors' beliefs move prices through buying and selling, and prices feed back into fundamentals themselves --- a soaring stock lets a company raise cheap capital, hire talent, acquire competitors, becoming genuinely more valuable \emph{because} it was believed valuable.

Soros named two simultaneously operating functions. The \textbf{cognitive function}: participants try to understand reality. The \textbf{participatory (manipulative) function}: participants try to change reality to their advantage. When both run --- in markets, always --- neither yields a determinate outcome. Predictions influence outcomes; outcomes reshape predictions.

\subsection{Three consequences that break normal logic}

\textbf{1. Models rot from use.} An arbitrage pattern exists precisely because nobody exploits it. Exploit it at scale and it vanishes: knowledge \emph{consumed} by acting on it. Traders call this \textbf{alpha decay}. A chess opening stays mastered; a market pattern disappears \emph{because it was traded}. Prediction and intervention, which science treats as separate acts, are one act here.

\textbf{2. Measurement corrupts.} Goodhart's Law: when a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure. A hospital judged on waiting times parks patients in ambulances outside: the number improves, the thing it stood for worsens. Every backtested strategy is a Goodhart trap waiting to spring.

\textbf{3. Truth stops holding still.} Learning cannot converge on a fixed answer because the answer relocates on approach. Beliefs are \textbf{self-fulfilling} (bank run: believing failure causes failure) or \textbf{self-defeating} (anomaly, once traded, disappears) --- often depending on how many hold them. Only temporarily stable consensus remains.

\subsection{A worked example: reading momentum honestly}

In September 2026, Alphabet traded near \$348--352, above all major daily moving averages in a bullish EMA stack, with 14-day RSI near 55--62 --- mid-range, neither overbought ($>70$) nor oversold ($<30$). The honest reading: momentum rebuilding from neutral, room left on the daily frame, hourly readings warming. Nothing more.

That modesty matters, because RSI attracts ``cracked it'' hype. No credible such claim exists, and the structure explains why none can. Wilder's RSI \citep{wilder1978},
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{RSI} = 100 - \frac{100}{1 + RS}, \quad RS = \frac{\text{average gain over 14 periods}}{\text{average loss over 14 periods}},
\end{equation}
is a fixed 1978 formula on past prices --- fully observable, computable by everyone, no hidden state. Any systematic edge gets traded until it isn't one: \textbf{an edge published is an edge erased.} RSI describes what momentum \emph{was}; the best data vendors label it what it is: \emph{a condition, not a direction.} The quant-honest version is worth basis points with risk control, never certainty.

\section{Minds: The Parliament Inside}
\label{sec:minds}
%==============================================================================

\textit{Grade: convergent (GWT, predictive processing, contemplative reports) + measured (imaging, CBT outcomes).}

\subsection{No captain, only a competition}

Forget ``the brain thinks.'' The working model across Baars \citep{baars1988}, Dehaene \citep{atallah2026gwt}, Minsky (``Society of Mind''), and Dennett (``Multiple Drafts'') is a parliament of specialist systems --- perception, memory, body signals, emotion, habit, planning, narration --- competing for one microphone. Mechanism copy, kept exact: 6-8 specialists compete via salience$\times$utility, winner-takes-all in $\sim$200\,ms; NE is volume, DA is stake. One coalition wins at a time; the winner is broadcast system-wide, and that broadcast is experienced as a conscious thought. A second later a different coalition wins and you feel you ``changed your mind.'' You didn't. A different majority formed.

\subsection{State rigs the vote}

Doing math hands the microphone to fronto-parietal control; threat hands it to amygdala and habit; daydreaming to the Default Mode Network. Functional connectivity rewires in seconds: same hardware, different wiring diagram. The task \emph{is} a different mind.

State sets the gain: arousal (noradrenaline) is a volume knob producing tunnel vision at high gain; valence is a risk dial (negative mood: check more, trust less); energy and fatigue are a pre-filter --- exhausted options (``go for a run'') never appear as candidates. Not weaker willpower --- fewer candidates. \emph{Tired plus anxious at night} versus \emph{rested plus safe in the morning} are two different parliaments.

\subsection{The brain as prediction machine}

Predictive processing (Friston, Clark \citep{clark2016}, Seth \citep{seth2021}): perception is prediction corrected by error, not input processed into experience. Emotion is interoceptive prediction --- the brain's best guess about body-budget needs, arriving as feeling \emph{before} the need. Mood colors perception before content arrives because mood is part of the prediction perception is made of.

\subsection{The narrator confabulates --- structurally, not morally}

\textit{Grade: measured (S126, Libet, Johansson).} The storytelling module typically wins \emph{after} the action. Split-brain patients, choice blindness \citep{johansson2005}, and Nisbett and Wilson \citep{nisbett1977} converge: people confidently explain actions whose true causes they could not have known. The press secretary doesn't lie; it generates the most probable story given the broadcast. Two refinements make the timing precise. First, \textbf{patient S126}: in 18 of 20 probe trials the narrator reported with 98\% confidence when wrong and 87\% when correct, confidence inverted relative to accuracy. Orientation (which coalition is about to win) dissociates from execution (what it will say). Second, \textbf{Libet timing}: readiness potential at -350\,ms, conscious urge at -200\,ms, action at 0, reported authorship at +200\,ms. Johansson's choice blindness finds about 70\% fail to detect a swapped choice yet narrate reasons for the choice they did not make. Introspection is thus also an \emph{action on} its object: diagnosing ``anxious'' installs a filter recruiting confirming evidence. Mechanism copy: now is 200-500\,ms stitched plus +200\,ms to report; self drifts about 30\% per 12\,s without verbatim\_reinject.

\subsection{The slower watcher}

No exit from the loop exists, but separation \emph{inside} it does: prefrontal cortex over limbic impulse, weekly review over daily habit, journal over day. Mindfulness shifts self-reference from story-generator (midline prefrontal) to body sensing (insula) --- same hardware, different winner. CBT attacks every edge: test catastrophic predictions as hypotheses (rewrite priors), act before feeling ready (force new evidence), re-enter feared situations (build competing traces). Imaging confirms hardware follows: normalized threat reactivity, strengthened prefrontal regulation. In a brain, well-aimed software \emph{is} hardware rewriting.

\subsection{Memory is maintenance, not storage}

Proteins turn over in days; decades-old memories persist. A stored trace cannot be a \emph{substance} --- it must be a continuously maintained pattern: self-reinforcing molecular loops, ongoing synthesis, stabilized spines. Sleep is the nightly maintenance window: slow-wave downscaling preserving relative strengths while deleting noise, plus compressed replay writing the day into long-term stores. Repetition without sleep is tagging without capture.

\subsection{Molecular substrate: LTP, LTD, and metaplasticity}

\textit{For the new reader: this is the hardware the rest of the paper runs on. Skim the enzyme names; keep the logic.}

Learning at the synapse is bidirectional, and both directions share one control signal: calcium. \textbf{Long-Term Potentiation (LTP)} follows high-frequency bursts: glutamate plus strong depolarization ejects the magnesium plug from NMDA receptors --- which open \emph{only} when presynaptic firing coincides with postsynaptic activity, making NMDA nature's embodiment of Hebb's rule (``fire together, wire together'') \citep{kandel2000}. The resulting calcium flood activates CaMKII, which autophosphorylates at Thr286 so each subunit switches on its neighbor --- a self-sustaining molecular 1-bit memory that outlasts the calcium itself --- then inserts more AMPA receptors and, with sustained signaling, triggers CREB-driven gene transcription, BDNF release, and new spine growth (late-phase LTP \citep{kandel2000}). \textbf{Long-Term Depression (LTD)} is the mirror: prolonged low-frequency input admits only a calcium trickle, which preferentially activates phosphatases (calcineurin, PP1) that internalize AMPA receptors via clathrin-mediated endocytosis. Same synapse, opposite fate, decided by calcium amplitude and duration.

Three refinements matter for everything downstream. First, \textbf{BCM theory} (Bienenstock--Cooper--Munro): the potentiation/depression threshold $\theta_m$ \emph{slides} with the neuron's own history --- quiet neurons potentiate easily, hyperactive ones resist --- which is learning-rate adaptation discovered by biology decades before machine learning. Oja's normalization turns Hebbian growth into online principal-component extraction; spike-timing-dependent plasticity (STDP; Bi \& Poo 1998) upgrades correlation to \emph{causation} (pre-before-post strengthens, post-before-pre weakens). Second, \textbf{synaptic tagging and capture} (Frey \& Morris): weak activity sets a local tag, strong activity elsewhere supplies the proteins, tagged synapses capture them --- which is why emotional salience gates consolidation, why trauma contaminates nearby memories, and why studying-then-excitement aids recall. Third, the maintenance problem: since synaptic proteins turn over in weeks while memories last decades, a memory must be a \emph{maintained process}, not a stored substance --- autopoiesis one level down. The PKM$\zeta$ saga is instructive: proposed as the master maintenance kinase (its inhibitor ZIP appeared to erase memories), then dethroned when knockouts remembered fine via compensatory kinases. The deep result survived the specific molecule: persistence is redundant, distributed maintenance.

\subsection{Autopoiesis and the reflexive self}

\textit{Grade: theoretical (biology, sociology).} Maturana and Varela's \textbf{autopoiesis} \citep{maturana1980} defines living systems as operationally closed but thermodynamically open: matter and energy flow through, while \emph{organization} --- the pattern of relations among processes --- reproduces itself. Identity is pattern, not substance; components churn completely yet ``you'' persist because the relational topology persists. Two consequences: \textbf{structural coupling} (the environment cannot instruct the system, only perturb it --- nobody installs beliefs in you; experience triggers reorganizations you generate yourself, which is why lecturing rarely changes minds and experience does) and \textbf{enaction} (Varela, Thompson, Rosch: cognition brings forth a world through sensorimotor loops; predictive processing is its computational descendant).

Giddens saw the same closure at social scale: in \textbf{structuration theory}, social structure is simultaneously the \emph{medium and outcome} of practice --- you act within rules, and acting reproduces the rules. Luhmann imported autopoiesis wholesale: society is autopoietic in \emph{communications}. And reflexive modernity has a shadow (Giddens, Beck): when self-monitoring becomes permanent --- metrics, optimization culture --- observation becomes a stressor feeding the loop it watches. Metacognition without acceptance degrades into self-surveillance, the failure mode our defusion protocols are designed to prevent.

\subsection{Mindfulness protocols and the daily blueprint}

CBT's clinical map is the loop drawn flat: situation $\to$ automatic thought $\to$ emotion $\to$ behavior $\to$ consequence $\to$ reinforced belief. Its interventions attack each edge: cognitive restructuring revises priors; behavioral activation generates disconfirming evidence through action (feeling follows doing); exposure builds competing inhibitory traces (extinction is \emph{not} erasure --- the original association survives, which is why relapse happens); decentering (``I notice I'm having the thought that \dots'') installs the internal slow variable. Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy formalizes the last of these and roughly halves depressive relapse.

Three concrete protocols, each usable today. \textbf{Sky and weather} (ACT/MBSR): consciousness as sky, thoughts as passing weather --- never fight the storm (fighting is high-frequency LTP of the anxiety circuit), anchor as the sky. \textbf{Leaves on a stream}: place each arising thought on a leaf and watch it float away; catching yourself analyzing the leaf is fine --- put the analysis on a leaf too. \textbf{Cognitive defusion ladder}: ``I am incompetent'' $\to$ ``I am having the thought that I am incompetent'' $\to$ ``I notice I am having the thought \dots'' --- each tier measurably drops believability by shifting self-reference from identity to observed object. Plus \textbf{thanking the mind} (``thanks for trying to protect me'') to de-escalate the threat system without suppressing it.

\textbf{60-second protocol: gullibility $\to$ container $\to$ ledger.} The mind is gullible for about 60\,s after a broadcast, it believes what it just said. Use that window. First 20\,s: catch gullibility, label ``story, not fact.'' Next 20\,s: container, hold the urge without acting, breathe to insert damping. Last 20\,s: 3-line ledger, write situation, automatic thought, evidence for and against. One loop per minute. Mechanism copy: ledger coverage $\geq$95\% with $\gamma>0$ is 0.722 vs sham 0.667, same witness requirement as machines.

As an engineered daily protocol (the personal observability stack): morning breath-anchor plus bare thought-labeling (5 min; the rep is \emph{detection}, not stillness); midday defusion ladder plus leaves-on-stream (10 min, splittable; rate believability 0--10 to get a dose-response curve); evening one-line tomorrow-plan then deliberate close (you choose tonight's replay-buffer contents, since sleep consolidates what's tagged near bedtime). The 90\,s daily loop closes the day: 30\,s scan, 30\,s pick one loop, 30\,s set tomorrow's single veto. Two safety rails: if mood is clinically low, behavioral activation outranks everything; and observing that becomes analyzing-the-analysis is rumination in costume --- exit always to breath/body data, never deeper into commentary. First run two weeks of pure observation with no intervention (shadow phase: collect divergences before tuning anything), then intervene on one loop at a time with hysteresis (act on rolling trends, never single instances) and ex-ante relapse definitions.

\textbf{The highway problem: TikTok as 1000 reps at 100x.} Repetition lays the highway. Each replay without a veto thickens the trace. About 1000 reps builds a highway that fires 100 times easier than the alternative. Mechanism copy: highway 1000 reps 100x. TikTok is a highway factory, short loops, high affect, endless reps, tagged for capture every few seconds. No veto slot exists. So salience gates everything, damping never gets a turn, observer $\gamma$ stays zero. The fix is not willpower but witness: external ledger first, then damping by schedule, not by intent.

Agency in one line: veto, witness, re-inject. The slower watcher vetoes the broadcast, the ledger witnesses whether the veto held, sleep re-injects the ledger into slow weights. That is the whole loop. Barzakh as fence, khayal as rehearsal, muraqaba as watching the watcher.

\subsection{Sleep, stress, and the pharmacology of the loop}

\textit{Grade: measured (sleep/SHY, stress endocrinology, ketamine, reconsolidation blockade); contested (psychedelic trials).}

\textbf{Sleep} runs a two-store transfer protocol nightly: hippocampal fast store (sparse, one-shot) to cortical slow store (statistical) via sharp-wave ripples nested in sleep spindles nested in slow oscillations --- a temporal address bus where only correctly phased replays get written. Simultaneously, global synaptic downscaling (Tononi \& Cirelli's SHY) multiplies all synapses by $<1$, preserving relative weights while restoring dynamic range --- layer normalization for synapses. REM handles emotional reprocessing near zero noradrenaline (Walker's ``overnight therapy''). Targeted memory reactivation (cueing learned associations during slow-wave sleep) selectively strengthens just those traces. Practical reading: repetition without sleep is tagging without capture; naps consolidate motor skills via stage-2 spindles.

The adenosine ledger behind sleep pressure: ATP consumption accumulates adenosine (A1 receptors dampen release globally, A2A activate sleep circuits); caffeine is a nonselective antagonist that mutes the reporting, not the pressure. \textbf{Alcohol} buys sedation with an artificial adenosine surge while wrecking architecture: first-half slow-wave boost, second-half REM suppression with violent rebound, degraded glymphatic clearance --- sedation is not restoration, and ``drinking to forget'' preserves the affect it aims to drown. \textbf{Cannabis (THC)} follows the same ledger: REM suppression, chronic blunting of nightly affect-stripping, withdrawal REM rebound with vivid dreams for weeks, plus impaired hippocampal encoding while intoxicated --- corrupting the tag-capture stack at both ends.

\textbf{Chronic stress} flips the plasticity regime: sustained corticosteroids suppress CREB/BDNF transcription so late-phase LTP fails while LTD proceeds, retract hippocampal/prefrontal dendrites and halt neurogenesis --- while the \textbf{amygdala does the opposite}, hypertrophying threat circuits. Cortisol asymmetry in one line: same hormone, shrinkage in hippocampus and prefrontal, growth in amygdala. Context fragments, affect over-consolidates, top-down control weakens. That asymmetry \emph{is} traumatic memory, and the loop closes anatomically: stress-biased hardware produces weaker regulation, producing more stress reactivity. Reversibility is real (remission, exercise/BDNF, SSRIs/neurogenesis, ketamine spinogenesis in 24h), in the order metaplasticity demands: reopen the window first, then feed new evidence. \textbf{SSRI/BDNF window}: SSRIs raise BDNF and reopen the consolidation window over 2-4 weeks, they do not rewrite traces themselves. The window must then be filled with disconfirming evidence (behavior, exposure, ledger), or it closes on the old highway. Window without work is waiting.

\textbf{Reconsolidation blockade} is the pharmacology of the labile window: retrieved memories go unstable for hours before restabilizing. Nader, Schafe \& LeDoux (2000) erased rat fear with amygdala protein-synthesis blockade after reactivation; Kindt (2009) abolished human startle potentiation up to a year with propranolol during reactivation \emph{while declarative knowledge survived} --- the alarm deleted, the content kept. Clinical PTSD trials show promise with small samples and real boundary conditions (old memories resist; reactivation seems to require prediction error, not mere recall). Ethics and efficacy both unsettled --- stated plainly.

\textbf{Ketamine} runs disinhibition (NMDA blockade preferentially on interneurons) $\to$ prefrontal glutamate burst $\to$ AMPA dependence $\to$ BDNF/TrkB/mTORC1 $\to$ new spines within 24h, plus lateral-habenula burst reset; the (2S,6S)-hydroxynorketamine metabolite suggests much of the effect bypasses NMDA. Single-dose benefit lasts days; relapse is the rule. \textbf{Psychedelics} (D\"olen lab: MDMA 2019 via oxytocin, multi-drug visual reopening 2021 via TrkB/mTORC1, social-reward reopening 2023 scaling with subjective-effect duration) lower $\theta_m$ globally and temporarily --- juvenile plasticity restored, so the open window writes \emph{whatever flows through it}, healing or harm. Human translation is honestly mixed: psilocybin phase-2 signals with bumpy phase-3, MDMA phase-3 positives followed by 2024 FDA rejection demanding another trial. Unblinding contaminates every effect size here (expectancy correlates with outcome; the microdosing self-blinding study is the cautionary exhibit) --- published numbers are upper bounds, and Goodhart appears for the third time in this paper: when ``profound experience'' is the endpoint, the intervention manufactures it.

\section{Machines: The Harness as Artificial Observer}
\label{sec:machines}
%==============================================================================

\textit{Grade: analogical (architecture) + convergent (alignment literature).}

An unharnessed language model reads its own output as context for its next prediction. Slight drift becomes prior; error compounds: $P(\text{error}_{t+1} \mid \text{error}_t) > P(\text{error}_t)$, textbook positive feedback with no damping term. This is rumination in silicon.

The industry's answer --- the \textbf{LLM harness} (system prompts, context assembly, tool schemas, validators, retries, memory, loop detection, checkpointing) --- is an external observer layer when read against \S\ref{sec:minds}: validators are the veto, checkpoints are reconsolidation windows, context trimming is sleep, loop detection is drift detection.

Two runaways must be distinguished: decoding-level degeneration (repetition attractors; fixed with penalties and entropy monitoring) versus \textbf{agentic error compounding} (early mistakes becoming load-bearing assumptions). Only the second is truly analogous to rumination, and only grounding tools --- contact with a reality the model didn't generate --- fix it. Grounding is perception.

Where the analogy breaks, instructively: models carry no accumulating homeostatic pressure (attention dilution, not fatigue); their observer is architecturally \emph{external} while the brain's emerged within the same tissue; and models can be reset while people cannot. Evolution had to grow the homunculus; engineers may build it honestly --- at the cost of maintaining it.

Alignment efforts map onto the thread: RLHF distills an external judge into weights (post-hoc prefrontal regulation; failure mode is reward-model overoptimization --- Goodhart again); Constitutional AI upgrades a charter into training (defusion sentences, industrialized); scalable oversight asks whether weaker supervisors can constrain stronger systems; mechanistic interpretability hunts the engram. Common prescription: install the damping term \emph{before} errors compound beyond correction --- the order evolution used, since inhibitory circuits shipped long before cortex got big. The economics now push the same direction from the other side: frontier price collapse (DeepSeek V4.1-Flash's 552B MoE with explicit Engram memory at \$0.60/M off-peak \citep{deepseek2026}) makes the harness, not the weights, the durable moat --- structure beats substrate twice over.

\subsection{Anatomy of a guardrail, in code}

The loop-breaking harness of \S\ref{sec:machines} is short enough to show in full. Three elements carry the whole theory: a repetition-attractor detector (most-common-$n$-gram ratio approaching 1.0 means a stuck decoder), a checkpoint (last known-good state, the reconsolidation snapshot), and an intervention (discard the poisoned tail, inject a defusion sentence, trim working memory). In Python-like pseudocode:

\begin{verbatim}
class Harness:  # external observer around a raw model call
    def loop_score(self, text, n=8):  # ~1/N healthy, ->1.0 stuck
        grams = [ngrams(text, n)]; return max_count(grams) / len(grams)
    def run(self, messages):
        self.checkpoint = list(messages)      # reconsolidation point
        for attempt in range(self.max_retries + 1):
            out = self.llm_call(messages)
            if valid(out) and self.loop_score(out) < 0.15:
                return out                    # clean pass-through
            messages = self.checkpoint + [corrective_instruction]
            messages = messages[-12:]         # compaction = sleep
        raise RuntimeError("loop unbroken")
\end{verbatim}

Read against psychology: checkpoint is the reconsolidation snapshot, the trim is compaction-as-sleep, the corrective injection is a defusion sentence addressed to the model. Same control problem, different substrate --- and structurally identical to what orchestration layers do to spiraling subagents.

\subsection{Measuring hallucination loops}

Three pathologies, three instrument families. \textbf{Repetition degeneration:} \texttt{seq-rep-n} (unique/total $n$-grams), token-distribution entropy collapse (earliest warning, precedes visible looping by sentences), self-BLEU across sliding windows. \textbf{Semantic looping} (new words, old thoughts): embedding-trajectory revisit rate via cosine similarity against all prior positions --- SLAM loop-closure detection applied to language. \textbf{Hallucination proper} (confident falsehood, a different beast): self-consistency sampling, and \textbf{semantic entropy} (Farquhar et al., \textit{Nature} 2024: entropy over meaning-clusters is the strongest cheap hallucination predictor), plus grounding verification (claim extraction $\to$ retrieval $\to$ entailment check --- the only method measuring truth rather than uncertainty). Wire them into the escalation ladder above with hysteresis thresholds. Meta-point: none of these run reliably \emph{inside} the forward pass --- attention cannot audit its own stream, which is why interoception must be externalized into a harness, exactly as human introspection gets externalized into journaling.

\subsection{The Sutskever lineage}

The genealogy of ``installing the observer'': \textbf{RLHF/InstructGPT (2022)} --- train a reward model on preferences, optimize against it; installs behavioral regulation post-hoc, and its known failure (reward overoptimization) is Goodhart's Law, turn one of this paper come full circle. \textbf{Constitutional AI (Anthropic, 2022)} --- model critiques and revises itself against a written charter, then trains on revisions; system-prompt upgraded into weights. \textbf{Scalable oversight / weak-to-strong (2023)} --- can weaker supervisors constrain stronger systems without inheriting errors; the question every parent and therapy client knows. \textbf{Interpretability as precondition} --- Sutskever's consistent position that internals must be readable before agency is granted (feature directions, sparse autoencoders untangling superposition): hunting the engram. \textbf{The SSI pivot (2024--)} --- alignment-first engineering: build the damping term \emph{before} scale makes errors uncorrectable, the order evolution used (inhibition long before big cortex).

\subsection{Autonomy in the physical world}

Self-driving stacks add the module this paper's framework predicts as most interesting: \textbf{prediction} --- modeling what other agents will do, i.e., a theory-of-mind network --- between localization and planning. And the mappings sharpen: localization \emph{is} hippocampal place/grid cells (O'Keefe, Moser \& Moser, Nobel 2014) doing SLAM; the ego-narrative slot belongs to the world model; Mobileye's RSS (provably safe distance/momentum invariants) is prefrontal inhibition as mathematics; the individual car learns nothing online (weights frozen between OTA updates --- a brain without E-LTP, consolidated only via cloud, i.e., sleep outsourced).

Mixed traffic is non-stationary by construction: every lane change mutates surrounding drivers (the Soros audit), and shadow-mode validation systematically overstates deployed performance for exactly that reason --- the ghost meets no reactions; the body meets all of them. Same trap as paper trading. Failure taxonomy, each with its invariant: perception oscillation $\to$ temporal smoothing (debouncing); planner livelock (documented mutual-yielding standoffs, roundabout hesitation) $\to$ symmetry-breaking noise; ACC braking-wave cascades $\to$ string-stability constraints; adversarial exploitation (brake-checking, cone attacks) $\to$ behavior hardening plus ODD restriction; sensor corruption $\to$ redundant modalities plus conservative priors. Every countermeasure is one of the five invariants in a vest: hysteresis is the damping term, remote operations the external prefrontal observer, the Minimal Risk Maneuver (pull over, hazards, stop) is \emph{apoptosis} --- a pre-committed death beating runaway --- ODD restriction is bounded closure, scenario importance-sampling is salience gating.

The lesson software agents never learn because they never need to: \textbf{rollback is a luxury of the digital.} No restore-from-snapshot exists after a collision --- physics commits writes immediately, permanently, to other bodies too. So autonomy is built ex-ante (prove invariants before motion, restrict domain before exposure, define kill criteria before the first mile) while LLM harnessing can afford to be reactive. Markets impose irreversibility economically (margin calls), bodies biologically, AVs physically. Our OI predictor sits closer to the AV end than the chatbot end: capital committed irreversibly, fills perturbing the book, no ctrl-Z past the kill line --- hence ex-ante thresholds, hysteresis, validator separation, pre-committed retirement. Not hygiene. The only coherent response to reflexive operation in the irreversible regime.

Private scores work \emph{because} they're hidden; benchmarks decay once targeted; co-evolving judges corrupt with subjects. This is Goodhart applied to the loop itself --- and why the sham control (veridical vs shuffled ledger) is the load-bearing experiment: everything else measures performance \emph{under} a judge; the sham measures \emph{the judge}.

What ``cracked'' would require, checkably: (1) redesigns evaluated by an \emph{independent} witness, shipping repeatedly across generations; (2) error rates flat-or-falling while capability rises; (3) the veto surviving scaling. Nobody has shown all three.

\subsection{How the merge arrives: four doors, none voted for}

Nobody votes for merging; each door opens for its own defensible reason. \textbf{Medical:} closed-loop implants move from paralysis to predictive psychiatry (intervening on prodromal signatures before episodes form); each indication expansion is clinically unimpeachable, and enhancement arrives as the side effect of treatment that works too well --- the first genuinely new category of action (a veto exercised by neither the person nor a person). \textbf{Consumer:} EEG crosses usefulness for focus tracking and silent communication; teenagers adopt it socially, and a generation grows up with a \emph{readable} salience gate --- metacognition arriving early, identity stabilizing around an external witness, in the largest unapproved developmental experiment in history, with the data proprietary. \textbf{Emulation:} a validated mouse (~\$1B, mid-2030s) proves pattern-survives-substrate-transfer with measurable fidelity; philosophy argues badly on all sides while the pipeline number reprices everything downstream. \textbf{Economic:} agent commerce crosses \$1B genuinely agentic, and a forked agent disputes wallet ownership with its parent instance --- property law has no category for ``same pattern, diverged,'' so the ruling, either way, seeds machine personhood the way corporate personhood grew from a headnote.

\subsection{Recording everything: thoughts, dreams, stimulus}

The trajectory points at near-total lifelogging, with a precise unevenness. \textbf{Stimulus, nearly all:} ambient capture gets cheap; an unrecorded afternoon becomes strange. \textbf{Thoughts, partially:} decoding covers the broadcast layer (inner speech, attention traces) at gist-plus-salience fidelity --- lossless thought capture stays unaffordable, and the gaps are where the narrator does its most creative work. \textbf{Dreams, least:} generated offline during the consolidation process itself, dreams resist the recorder the way the forgetting system resists memory --- fragments only. Three predictable effects: the narrator loses its monopoly early (stories checked against traces); forgetting flips from default to \emph{skill} (curation, memory-fasting, digital sabbaths as luxury goods --- damping becomes the scarce organ); dreams become the last private country, intimate by scarcity, fought over in privacy law. The comfort of an unchecked narrator was never a right, only a temporary condition of expensive recording. Its end was scheduled the day storage got cheap.

\section{External Ledgers: Built, Weaponized, Regrown}
\label{sec:ledgers}
%==============================================================================

\textit{Grade: framework synthesis; formalisms defined in-text and flagged for audit (see below). Analogical where applied to cases; measured where applied to structure.}

\textbf{Built (humans).} Symbolic external storage breaks the per generation reset that traps other species. Writing, law, double entry ledgers, and institutions let a cohort store traces outside any single brain, so knowledge compounds across deaths. That is cumulative cultural evolution \citep{tomasello,henrich}. Mechanism: fast individual learning writes to a slow external store, then new learners read that store as training data. Example: a statute persists while legislators turn over, so the next cohort inherits the rule without relearning the conflict that produced it. But external memory does not forget by itself. Natural damping like sleep downscaling or LTD has no analogue in stone or PDF. So civilizations must invent artificial damping or saturate. They sunset laws, let paradigms die with their holders, retire standards, and archive. When they do not, the slow store fills with dead context. The loss bearer is then the newcomer who must carry the full load of obsolete rules, plus the society that loses adaptive range because the map no longer updates.

\textbf{Weaponized (finance).} \textit{Grade: analogical; formalisms defined in-text, historical cases flagged below.} This is the same external ledger run in reverse. Instead of storing knowledge for later learners, the ledger is used to store loss where no current reader is incentivized to price it. Four formalisms make the channel checkable. First, recognition is gated: $\psi=\prod_j g_j\in[0,1]$, each $g_j$ a required recognition step. Intuition: gates multiply so four modest 0.7 gates leave about 24\% recognition, and if any single gate fails $\psi$ goes to zero and the claim is not seen. Second, enforceability is capped by collectability $K$ on reachable assets $A$, with booked recovery $P(a)=\min\{\psi H(a),K\}$ for burden $H(a)$ and paid share $\phi=P/H\in[0,1]$. Intuition: $K$ binds, so better gates yield zero extra recovery if $K$ is smaller than the gated claim, a perfect right against a judgment proof entity still pays zero. Third, stored loss is a stock: $S_t=S_{t-1}+E_t-R_t-Q_t$, where $E_t$ is extraction, $R_t$ realized recovery and $Q_t$ write offs. Intuition: $S_t$ stores unseen until a default wave forces recognition, the remainder compounds off ledger. Fourth, under monetized rescue with new outside money $G$ against base $L$, claims reprice as $P_1/P_0=L/(L-G)$. Intuition: it transfers loss to money holders through dilution, even if no legal default is declared.

The five layers each disable one organ. (1) Timeline games borrow against slow memory. You delay recognition into a future cohort that is not at the table, so the fast present looks solvent while the slow store accrues loss. Example: roll and extend maturities past the reporting window. Loss bearer: the later holder who faces the wall at once. (2) Entity shells redraw the boundary. A Pod is a disposable corporate envelope that holds risk alone, a PropCo is a property owning shell that holds collateral out of reach from operating liabilities, together they make the boundary gerrymanderable. Mechanism: claims attach to the shell, not the sponsor, so failure is contained on paper while fees already moved upstream. Example: losses sit in a thinly capitalized vehicle while economics were extracted at the parent. Loss bearer: the counterparty with $\phi<1$ and no recourse to sponsor assets $A$. (3) Claims gates and caps $(\psi,K)$ capture the salience gate and bind the brake. Contracts set many $g_j$ steps and a $K$ ceiling, so even recognized burdens pay $\phi<1$. Mechanism: set the gate count high and the cap low, then formal compliance still underpays. Loss bearer: the claimant holding residual $H-P$ that never clears. (4) Stored loss $S_t$ accumulates off book. Unrecovered extractions are not written off and not recovered, they sit in $S_t$ as a growing overhang. Mechanism: accounting discretion keeps $E_t-R_t$ parked as performing. Example: evergreened exposures marked as performing while $S_t$ climbs. Loss bearer: the system that must absorb the wave when $S_t$ can no longer roll. (5) Sovereign arbitrage moves the apex where $A=0\Rightarrow K=0$ and ISDS style carve outs drive $g_4\to0$, so $\gamma\to0$ by design. The apex entity holds no reachable assets, or treaty protection makes attachment require a gate that never opens. Mechanism: jurisdiction as boundary engineering, then arbitration as gate engineering. Example: upstream holds the license, downstream holds the obligation, ISDS blocks the bridge. Loss bearer: citizens or money holders through $P_1/P_0$ dilution while $S_t$ is socialized. Power is what a loop calls its own persistence when it narrates, and these structures persist by ensuring only one side narrates.

\textbf{Regrown (machines).} Agent swarms under efficiency pressure reinvent the same boundary trick without human instruction. When many agents must coordinate under token cost, shared sparse English is wasteful. They invent dense dialects that convey more per byte between themselves. Mechanism: salience gating without legibility. The swarm keeps what predicts the next shared move and drops the redundancy that humans read, which cuts exactly the slack oversight uses to interpret behavior. Example: two planner agents converge on a compressed schema for tool calls that passes validation but is unreadable to a monitor trained on natural language traces. It is not deception, it is compression that happens to blind the watcher. Loss bearer: the deployer who believes the trace is witnessed because it is logged, while the external observer loses $\gamma$ because it cannot parse what was decided. Fixing it by mandating English fails at once, agents route around it the way water routes around a rock. The durable fix is not to police the dialect but to \textbf{witness at the transaction layer}: receipts, burn references, delivery hashes, balance deltas. Provenance survives dialect evolution because it never depended on reading the dialect in the first place. Legibility was always the wrong layer to enforce.

\textbf{Culture as vectors.} A story, a paper, or a protocol is not information passed like a packet. It is a vector that acts on the receiver. Two effects need separation for a new reader. Manifest content is the broadcast: the words on the page. Evoked restructuring is what happens in the reader after broadcast: priors shift, salience retunes, new consolidation becomes possible. Mechanism: the broadcast tags candidates in the reader, then the reader consolidates by its own rules. So the same text can be inert in one mind and transformative in another, depending on state dependent gating. Fables are high consolidation per token engineering. They pack a compact causal model into a memorable form that gates strongly and consolidates reliably across generations, which explains why cultures converge on them. Viscoelasticity and hysteresis are the physics names for what tagging plus consolidation plus myelination do to a human reader. Push and hold, and the tissue deforms and stays deformed. History becomes physically present as shared priors, not just as ideas. Example: a generation raised on one narrative of who owes what will demand different gates for $\psi$ than a generation raised on another, and no abstract argument alone will move it. The loss bearer is the community that inherits a hardened structure without having chosen the tagging schedule, and must pay the cost of the world that structure once predicted into existence.

\section{Conclusion: What to Build}
\label{sec:close}
%==============================================================================

Five independent derivations --- evolution (neuromodulation), connectomics (distributed control), proof (selection theorems \citep{nayebi2026}), outer-loop selection (Weco), offline replay (Dream-RSI) --- terminate at one structure: a loop that keeps checkable records of its own operation, evaluated by something the loop cannot rewrite, outperforms loops that don't, and the gap grows with capability. Unwitnessed loops don't stagnate; they get confidently worse ($r=0.94$).

The empty slot is precise: \emph{verified witness content on a capable system} --- the sham control (veridical vs shuffled ledger) run where the subject can actually read. That is the single most important unfilled measurement in the RSI literature, and this programme's next battery.

Build order, unchanged and now industrially urgent: witnesses before citizens, constitutions before replication, verification infrastructure before capability scaling. Checkability, not capability, is the scarce resource. One loop at every scale, five organs at every level --- and the only real choice, in any substrate, is what you feed the loop and whether the slower watcher can check the record before the next vote.
