# Source of Truth — The Ledger Principle v1

`paper_book/` (main.tex + chapters/) is the frozen canon for all agents.
`research/` (all 94 md files) is canon source material: every idea lives there first.
`bookv2/` is a retelling. The retelling may change voice, never facts.

## What v1 guarantees
- Spine: systems that persist do it by maintaining a checkable record,
 and whoever writes the record decides who pays.
- Every number traces to `experiments/lab_runs_falsify/` or a held primary.
- Every claim carries its grade: measured, convergent, analogical, open.
- Opposers ride alongside corroborators; corrections print, never hide.

## Rules for every agent
1. Read v1 and `research/` before writing v2. Quote numbers and grades from v1 only.
2. Never move a claim across grades (analogical to measured) for elegance.
3. New threads enter as analogical/open with pointers, never as findings.
4. One chapter file touched at a time. Build stays green
 (2x pdflatex, 0 errors, 0 undefined).
5. If v1 and a research note disagree, v1 wins until a patch note says why.

 ch05/ch06 carry notation early by necessity).
- Allegories lived outside the manuscript; now housed in appendix
 (Sec. allegories) with a prologue pointer.
