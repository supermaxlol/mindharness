# Plot — 3 Acts, Russian Doll Reveals

## Act I: The Invention of the Game (cognitive & cultural)
Hook: a brain is a dead end without an outside operating system.
Engine: cumulative culture, ratchet, fable-as-software, Tasmania + crows.
Trap: eliminate individual genius. Doll-reveal: the brain hits a memory
limit, which requires the cultural ledger.

## Act II: Rigging the Game (financial architecture)
Shift: ledgers secured survival; now the ledger is the territory to conquer.
Engine: Master Extraction Engine (timeline, entity, claims, stored loss,
sovereign). Heavy proofs kept, each paired with a physical analogy.
Trap: eliminate bad-apples/crash theories. Inequality and arbitrage are the
intended output of the legal ledger (A=0 implies K=0).
Doll-reveal: the financial ledger demands speeds faster than human thought,
which requires the autonomous ledger.

## Act III: The Game Plays Itself (AI & convergence)
Shift: humans too slow; the ledger starts writing itself.
Engine: machine dialects, trilemma, collapse suite, pillars, witness
triangulation, RSI ladder, sham empty slot.
Trap: eliminate comfort of human oversight (governance paradox).
Close: unified sentence + falsifiers + build order (witnesses before
citizens, constitutions before replication, verification before scaling).
