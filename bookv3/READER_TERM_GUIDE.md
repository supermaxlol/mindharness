# Reader’s Term Guide

A quick companion to *The Ledger Principle*. Definitions follow the book’s usage; the linked chapters provide the examples and sources.

### Ledger

A maintained record consulted to decide what happens next. Its power depends on readers, rules and actions. [Read the chapter](chapters/00_the_pattern_that_borrows_stuff.md).

### Keeper

The person or arrangement that preserves, selects, consults or amends a consequential record. [Read the chapter](chapters/13_who_gets_to_amend_the_record.md).

### Checkability

A working route from a consequential entry to evidence, a warranted correction and an authority able to act on it. [Read the chapter](chapters/13_who_gets_to_amend_the_record.md).

### Witness

A checking process that connects evidence to a better course. The book asks what it reads and what its finding changes. [Read the chapter](chapters/09_the_witness_that_could_not_read.md).

### Provenance

The origin of an entry and the earlier entries it depends on. These links let a reader inspect how apparently separate accounts are related. [Read the chapter](chapters/11_the_ledger_that_eats_its_own_records.md).

### Boundary

The line that determines what an account includes, which entity owes, or which source a process reads. [Read the chapter](chapters/04_the_company_that_could_disappear.md).

### Correction and amendment

An error can be corrected in an entry; amendment becomes effective when it reaches the later decision governed by that entry. [Read the chapter](chapters/13_who_gets_to_amend_the_record.md).

### Cumulative inheritance

A later learner begins from earlier attempts rather than paying every discovery cost again. Transmission needs a usable route. [Read the chapter](chapters/01_the_mark_that_outlived_its_maker.md).

### Map room

The shared tools, notation, examples and teaching through which an individual obtains routes no individual assembled alone. [Read the chapter](chapters/02_the_map_room.md).

### Compressed meaning and compressed scribbles

The same shorthand can summon a learned history for one reader and remain opaque to another. Explanation supplies the missing walk. [Read the chapter](chapters/08_the_story_you_complete.md).

### Manifest record

The words, events and relations available on the page. [Read the chapter](chapters/08_the_story_you_complete.md).

### Evoked experience

The scene, memories, guesses and feelings a reader constructs while encountering that page. [Read the chapter](chapters/08_the_story_you_complete.md).

### Retained model

What remains available after reading and may guide a later decision. [Read the chapter](chapters/08_the_story_you_complete.md).

### Recognition, reachable resources and paid share

Three separate questions in a claim’s route. Chapter 5 uses psi (ψ), K and phi (φ) to keep them apart. [Read the chapter](chapters/05_the_tollbooths.md).

### Stored loss

The gap between a recorded carrying value and expected recoverable value. Chapter 6 names it S and explains the valuation work needed to estimate it. [Read the chapter](chapters/06_the_cost_that_would_not_stay_buried.md).

### Replacement and accumulation

Replacement trains a successor on the latest generated collection; accumulation retains earlier collections alongside additions in the experiments discussed. [Read the chapter](chapters/11_the_ledger_that_eats_its_own_records.md).

### Readiness potential

Preparatory brain activity studied in movement tasks. Chapter 14 compares arbitrary and deliberate choices before drawing its agency argument. [Read the chapter](chapters/14_the_agency_left_to_us.md).

### Agency across time

Endorsing a direction, preparing conditions, acting, retaining the outcome and making an amendment reach the next attempt. [Read the chapter](chapters/14_the_agency_left_to_us.md).

### Implementation intention

A plan linking a recognizable situation to a specified action. [Read the chapter](chapters/14_the_agency_left_to_us.md).

### Practice of amendment

The proposed seven-day sequence: expectation and plan before acting, event and next amendment afterward, with a return to the prior entry. [Read the chapter](chapters/15_the_practice_of_amendment.md).

### The distinction to keep

A record preserves an account. Provenance shows where it came from. A witness tests a consequential entry. Checkability includes the route by which a warranted correction reaches action. The institutional chapters ask who has access to each step and who bears the cost when that route fails.
