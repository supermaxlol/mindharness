## Translation and inference

- **Photocopy:** loss of a faint instruction gives the reader a familiar omission. The model mechanism is finite sampling and training; models can also invent artifacts, unlike ordinary fading ink.
- **Card bag:** the probability of missing the exceptional card in 100 independent draws with replacement is `(99/100)^100 ≈ 0.366`. It is constructed arithmetic, not an observed model failure rate, and is not entered as empirical microdata.
- **Drawer and apprentice:** full storage differs from the examples actually encountered. The primary fixed-budget result includes a successful plateau; V3 does not turn reduced influence into certain disappearance.
- **Delivery average:** validation and representational variety answer different questions. No delivery experiment is claimed.
- **Library messenger:** dependent documents can look like several witnesses. The retrieval study concerns narrowing answers; the dependence argument does not assume that every generated reference is false.
- **Smaller-language speaker:** a prospective institutional consequence, not a measured victim from these experiments. It gives the loss a bearer and identifies which selection rule should be investigated.

The remaining inference is that consequential reuse needs a maintained path to correction: recoverable origin, usable reference cases, tested selection, and an amendment that reaches the next process. Provenance is one part of that path. No cited paper proves that a provenance label alone guarantees truth or is the unique possible remedy.
