| Proposed line or mechanism | Editorial decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| “You are the maintenance of the loop.” | **Keep** as a provocative philosophical formulation. | It locates agency in ongoing deliberation, action, feedback, and revision. It is not a laboratory measurement of the self. |
| Approximately 98% of the body's atoms are replaced in a year. | **Restore with attribution.** | Aebersold's isotope-based historical estimate is explicit in the Smithsonian source. Atomic exchange is different from whole-cell replacement. |
| “You don't choose whether to feed the loop—you only choose what you feed it.” | **Qualify.** | People do not choose every thought, sensation, circumstance, or available resource. Some inputs and responses can be influenced; control varies by person and context. |
| The conscious narrator can arrive after neural preparation; about 200 ms is a key timing result. | **State the result prominently.** | In [Libet's task](https://www.trans-techresearch.net/wp-content/uploads/2015/05/Brain-1983-LIBET.pdf), the reported urge was around **200 ms before movement**, after the onset of preparatory activity. [Johansson’s original choice-blindness experiment](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16210542/) separately demonstrates post hoc explanations of substituted choices. The original archive's “+200 ms after action” timeline merges distinct phenomena. |
| Rehearsal changes the future nervous system; myelin makes a roughly 100× speed contrast vivid. | **State the two findings together.** | Across fiber classes, conduction speeds can be roughly **1 versus 100 m/s** ([neuroscience source](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK10921/)). [Bacmeister](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9651929/) measured task-linked myelin change in mouse motor learning; [McKenzie](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6324726/) tested the causal importance of new myelin-forming cells. The 100× figure compares fibers, while practice-dependent remodeling is a separate experimental finding. |
| Two-minute micro-promises, cues, and a progress log. | **Offer as an experiment, not a cure.** | Implementation-intention and monitoring studies show average benefits, but they do not validate this exact routine or a guaranteed self-trust ratio. See [Gollwitzer and Sheeran](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0065-2601%2806%2938002-1) and [Harkin et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26479070/). |
| An “un-editable” ledger. | **Use a dated, correctable record instead.** | A permanent original entry plus appended correction preserves accountability and context. An uncorrectable mistake can make the record another source of false authority. |
| Make one's highest endorsed values the path of least resistance. | **Keep as the aspiration; narrow the promise.** | A person can sometimes lower friction for a valued action. Illness, obligations, poverty, disability, and other people can alter what is possible. |
