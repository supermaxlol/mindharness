## How the essay's form becomes book form

The essay's rhythm is **mechanism → evidence → meaning**. The published chapter's rhythm is **scene → sensible expectation → failure → mechanism → decisive evidence → person who pays → next unresolved question**. This gives the new reader enough of the failed walk to decode the finding. It also stops the manuscript from reading like a list of measurements.

The essay's grades remain in research notes and source notes. In the body, the writer speaks plainly and uses the grade only when a reader needs to know whether a figure is observed, a model's result, an interpretation, or an open horizon. The source appendix remains the book's checkable ledger; it need not interrupt every page with audit vocabulary.

## The discipline test for every finished chapter

1. Can a reader identify the consequential record and its keeper without knowing our five-job vocabulary?
2. Does one scene show how the record changes an actual next decision?
3. Is the strongest rival explanation given a fair trial and a visible failure?
4. Is the person who receives value or bears cost named, with a concrete unit of gain or loss?
5. Can the affected person or independent witness correct the entry in time for the correction to matter?
6. Does the chapter earn its final question, which only the next chapter can answer?

If a passage cannot answer at least one of these questions, it belongs in a note, a later chapter, or the research archive. The book's sentence remains the same throughout: **systems that persist do it by maintaining a checkable record, and whoever writes the record decides who pays.**
