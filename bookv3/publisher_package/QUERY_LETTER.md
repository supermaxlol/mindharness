# Query letter — The Ledger Principle

**Subject:** Nonfiction query: The Ledger Principle — complete manuscript, 50,323 words

Dear Agent,

A supplier can win recognition of every dollar it is owed and receive less than a third. A machine can perform a check faithfully while reading the wrong record. In both cases, the consequential question is who can make the account change what happens next.

I am seeking representation for *The Ledger Principle: Memory, Power, and Who Pays*, a complete work of general nonfiction at 50,323 words, or 56,388 including chapter source notes. It follows the record from humanity's inherited knowledge through financial institutions, stories and machine memory, then returns to personal agency and a practical method of amendment.

The book's central argument is that whoever writes the record decides who pays. A recipe lets a stranger inherit a discovery. A corporate structure identifies which company must answer a claim. A machine's retained history determines what its successor learns. Each chapter follows the strongest alternative explanation into a concrete event and asks whether a correction can reach the next decision.

Documented cases include KB Toys' payout, a bankruptcy supplier's allowed claim and actual check, the closing public account of the RBS rescue, and Josephine Hamilton's excluded explanation in the Post Office prosecution. Cognitive and AI research carry the argument into the reader and the machine. A two-chapter practical coda turns it into a seven-day routine without treating institutional constraints as failures of discipline.

The book will appeal to readers interested in the information-and-power questions of Yuval Noah Harari's *Nexus* and the institutional history of Paolo Zannoni's *Money and Promises*. Its particular focus is the enforceable route between evidence, a correction and its consequence.

My related writing examines selfhood, dependence and agency. For this manuscript I have assembled a research archive, primary-source notes and documented machine-agent tests. The complete manuscript, proposal and sample chapters are available.

Thank you for your consideration.

Shehzad Ahmed
