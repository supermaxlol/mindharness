# Author information for final tailoring

Internal worksheet; keep separate from materials sent to agents or editors.

The current proposal uses a project-based biography. Add confirmed details when supplied:

- Professional roles and qualifications relevant to the book.
- Publications, with publication status and links.
- Speaking, teaching or media experience.
- Current audience channels and dated reach figures.
- Contact email for submissions; location if desired.
- Any endorsement or partnership, with permission and actual status.

No degree, institutional affiliation, publication credit, audience figure or media commitment has been assumed. The query is a usable generic draft; personalize its recipient and add contact details before sending. Sending a letter requires the author's explicit instruction.
