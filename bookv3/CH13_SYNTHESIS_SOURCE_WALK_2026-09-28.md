## The theorem and the positive engineering result

[Nayebi, *What Capable Agents Must Know*](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v337/nayebi26a.html), [Theorem 5](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.02491#Thmtheorem5), was revisited through primary in-body passages. Under its no-aliasing setup, paired regret has a lower bound proportional to witnessed aliasing mass and a margin-dependent factor. Histories warranting opposite confident bets cannot repeatedly be merged by a policy achieving sufficiently low regret on those tests. The chapter gives the result its antecedent and uses the two jars as a constructed decoder.

This is the formal memory leg of the five-job convergence. The five jobs are assembled from that pressure, biological maintenance, legal pathways, and actual engineered tests. The chapter does not assign its exact five names to the theorem or infer phenomenal consciousness from it. The wider structural-task corollaries explain why task-block and regime distinctions can matter under their specified conditions.
