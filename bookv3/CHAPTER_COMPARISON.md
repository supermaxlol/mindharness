# Chapter-by-chapter V1 → V2 comparison

The two books share the same chapter spine. V2 adds short changes to twelve shared files, adds `ch08b_prayer.tex`, and leaves the four back-matter chapter files unchanged. This map identifies what to carry into V3 and what to rebuild.

| Existing chapter | Keep from V1/V2 | V2's meaningful change | V3 decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Preface | Authorial ambition and the need for source grades | Stronger direct address and admission that prior receipts were misread | Compress to one page after the prologue; avoid duplicate opening arguments |
| How to Read | Reader-facing definitions and grade system | Adds familiar *Memento* anchor and “three doors” | Move grade detail to notes; keep one worked example, no 2,000-word instruction manual before story |
| Prologue: Pattern | Strong image of persistence amid replacement | Adds trapdoor and maintenance language | Replace with documented 2010 accounting event; retain Ship of Theseus/whirlpool only where they clarify a later question |
| Human | Cumulative culture and external storage | Adds crow example | Keep inheritance as Act I; remove animal “reset to zero,” biological determinism, and forecasts masquerading as findings |
| Timeline | BofA consolidation filing and dividend-recap source trail | Two paragraphs sharpen the time-transfer framing | Separate gross issuance, leverage, recognition, and realized loss; follow one transaction through time |
| Entity | Bank VIE panel and boundary-focused court cases | Adds two objection paragraphs and a schematic “assembled blueprint” | Keep the best case; avoid treating assembled parts from different firms as one observed engine |
| Claims | Recovery data, priority, procedural gates | Minimal bridge and reader challenge | Follow one claimant; put stylized gate equation after the actual legal route |
| Stored Loss | BIS, FDIC, IMF, CPI, and default series | Minimal bridge and prose emphasis | Retain separately dated series; remove causal leap from bailout to a fixed price-level ratio |
| Sovereign | Two finance-related ISDS cases and dissent | One analogy-line adjustment | Trace treaty jurisdiction, award, recognition, and execution; state what domestic law can still do |
| Cognitive | Parliament, narrator, self-checking practices | Adds “three doors” and a stronger sham explanation | Rebuild around text → evoked simulation → retained change; use Aebersold's ~98% atom estimate, Libet's ~200 ms-before-movement result, choice blindness, the ~100× cross-fiber speed contrast, and motor-learning myelin studies accurately |
| Prayer (V2 only) | Sincere account of religious practice | Entire chapter new | Optional personal coda, outside the empirical proof chain, if the author wants it |
| Machine | Strongest original contrast: storage/reporting versus causal checking | Clarifies the human loss-bearer | Put the project's failed capable-subject check and 200-turn reversal in the foreground; distinguish experiments from future deployments |
| Unified | Tries to grade multiple lines of support and state falsifiers | Intensifies convergence rhetoric | Make five recurring jobs the earned cross-domain inference; let rival explanations fail in scenes before the synthesis is named |
| Appendix | Claim-to-file pointers and glossary | Unchanged | Rebuild as source notes and correction ledger; keep reader text uncluttered |
| Annotated bibliography | Broad route into literature | Unchanged | Verify each primary work and show which specific sentence it supports |
| Discussion questions / handbook | Actionable reader exercises | Unchanged | Turn into a one-page “five questions for any consequential record” tool, with one real worked example |

V1's best quality is the unusually large evidence trail. V2's best quality is its effort to translate that trail into a voice. V3's central editorial move is to make the evidence trail **causal and sequential**: one record, one user of it, one person harmed or helped, one available correction, one observed result. The ledger can be a protagonist without being declared a literal organism or a universal law.
