# The Ledger Principle — edition changes

**Author:** Shehzad Ahmed 
**Edition:** Revised reading edition, 13 October 2026 
**Earlier public reading edition:** 9 October 2026, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23252269 
**Version family:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23252268

The complete manuscript retains its prologue, fifteen chapters, future outlook and source notes. This is a targeted revision of Version 3, not a new book.

## What changed

- **Chapter 2 — The Map Room:** develops the distinction between preserving knowledge and making the means to use it available. Bose Institute's founding address and its 2026 internship notice support an institutional case. A clearly imagined apprentice distinguishes access to a text from access to supervised practice, tools and time. The chapter then separates productive contribution from rights over the proceeds.
- **Chapter 4 — The Company That Could Disappear:** clarifies that an enforceable route to another payer must still be examined for resources and claim priority.
- **Chapter 11 — The Ledger That Eats Its Own Records:** clarifies that a useful witness must be able to deliver a needed correction; a correct decision need not change merely to demonstrate oversight.
- **Chapter 13 — Who Gets to Amend the Record?:** develops practical access to correction through the Criminal Cases Review Commission's published guidance and a bounded qualitative study of applicant experience. Formal permission and usable investigative/legal support remain distinct.
- **Chapter 15 — The Practice of Amendment:** extends the worked proposal to temporary data access, authorized retention or continuing inspection, and a usable handoff to a successor. The existing four-line daily record and seven-day sequence remain intact.
- **Future Outlook:** extends the projected procurement test to loss of supplier access, retention and maintenance responsibilities. The final flood-mark scene now transmits the means of checking to an apprentice. The ten dated forecast rows remain unchanged.

## Reading and evidence

All seventeen sections and their source notes received a complete sequence review. The seven-day practice and procurement scenes remain constructed designs. Clinical cases, local simulations, formal results, published experiments and dated forecasts retain their separate evidential status. This revision does not report new experiments or claim that every previously cited source has been freshly audited.

The revised manuscript contains **51,230 main-text words** and **57,653 words including chapter source notes**, using whitespace-delimited counts. These totals exclude the contents, copyright page and this change log. The revised PDF includes an updated edition date and copyright page; its short practical table is kept intact for reading.
