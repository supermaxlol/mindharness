## 4. Direct-test short bullets added 2026-09-25 (paper-index verified, short)

**A. Viscoelastic / hysteresis formal models — substrate + formalism, not yet closed loop:**
- Smith, Roy, De Koninck, Grütter & De Koninck 2007 Biophys J (DOI 10.1529/biophysj.106.092361, PMID 17114228, PMC1783894) — AFM + FRAP on live spines; soft-glassy rheology, dynamic remodeling vs structural stability tradeoff. *Grade: measured (biophysics). Limit: no behavioral hysteresis.*
- Benna & Fusi 2016 Nat Neurosci 19:1697 (DOI 10.1038/nn.4401, PMID 27694992) — coupled chain (beaker-tube), bidirectional fast→slow transfer, 1/t and 1/√t power-law forgetting, history-dependent consolidation, spacing/metaplasticity. *Grade: theoretical (formal, simulated).*
- Bonilla-Quintana & Rangamani 2024 eNeuro 11(3) ENEURO.0497-23.2024 (DOI 10.1523/ENEURO.0497-23.2024, PMID 38383589) — 3D moving-boundary actin/Arp2/3/cofilin model, perisynaptic mechanics enhance LTP enlargement, adaptation to repeated stimuli (force-velocity nonlinearity). *Grade: theoretical (computational).*
- Ziegler, Zenke, Kastner & Gerstner 2015 J Neurosci 35:13103 (DOI 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.3989-14.2015, PMID 25609644) — three-timescale synaptic consolidation, induction (s) → hours → years, behavioral savings after consolidation. *Support: theoretical, convergent with Benna-Fusi; kept as adjacent, not primary hysteresis test.*

**B. Longitudinal newcomer-inheritance of machine dialects — generational turnover measured:**
- Li & Bowling 2019 arXiv:1906.02403 — neural sender-receiver referential game, new agents replace old periodically (sequential + population), ease-of-teaching pressure structures emergent code. *Grade: measured (pre-LLM). N=population chains, not single pair.*
- Kouwenhoven, Peeperkorn & Verhoef 2024 arXiv:2412.07646 — LLM-LLM iterated learning of artificial languages (classical referential game), holistic→structured over generations, learnability ↑. *Grade: measured (LLM, generational). Limit: degenerate vocab in some runs (honesty flag).*
- Guo, Wu & Yiu 2026 arXiv:2605.23054 — LLaMA-2-7B & Mistral-7B self-training ×10 generations EN/DE/TR, 5 falsifiable iterated-learning predictions, non-monotonic compositionality (rise then fall), BF10>100, R²=0.94 vs human regularization gradient. *Grade: measured (strongest discriminant; persistence only with task-grounded filtering).*
- Perez, Kovač et al 2024 arXiv:2407.04503 — telephone-game transmission chains with LLM agents, toxicity/positivity/length attractors amplify over multi-step chains, instruction-dependence. *Grade: measured (adjacent: content attractors, not code lexicon; kept as convergent for dialect interpretation).*
- Prior convergent base retained: Kirby lab cumulative evolution + ECOCURL paradigm + Kottur et al 2017 arXiv:1706.08502 non-emergence constraint (natural language does not emerge “naturally”; bounds naive emergence claims). *Grade: convergent/constraint, not upgraded.*

**Grading honesty:** No claim that any single paper directly closes “viscoelastic spine memory = behavioral hysteresis” — Smith gives substrate, Benna-Fusi gives formalism, Bonilla-Quintana gives computational bridge. For dialect, longitudinal newcomer-inheritance is now measured at LLM scale (Guo 2026 discriminant, Kouwenhoven 2024 generational), pre-LLM measured (Li 2019), with human analogue convergent (Kirby).
