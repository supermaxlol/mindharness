 TROUBLED ASSET RELIEF PROGRAM: Lifetime Cost

 GAO-24-107033, December 2023

 On October 3, 2008, Congress passed the Emergency Economic Stabilization Act of 2008 (EESA) in response to the
 financial crisis. EESA established the Office of Financial Stability (OFS) within the Department of the Treasury and
 created the Troubled Asset Relief Program (TARP) to assist financial institutions and markets, businesses,
 homeowners, and consumers. Over the past 15 years, OFS managed the TARP investments to minimize costs to the
 taxpayers. With the conclusion of TARP in September 2023, we can now summarize the ultimate cost of the program.
