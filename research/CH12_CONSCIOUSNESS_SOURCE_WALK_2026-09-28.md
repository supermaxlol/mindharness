## Two source refinements that matter

1. **Unresponsive is not interchangeable with unconscious.** The appended comparison packet describes ketamine and propofol as “both unconscious” while using their metabolic differences against a theory. Sarasso's finding instead separates behavioral unresponsiveness from dream-associated experience. That shorthand cannot establish that the two states have the same experiential status, or falsify a whole biological-computational theory. V3 retains the stronger positive dissociation.
2. **Local witness failure does not settle subjective experience.** The consciousness appendix contains older summaries of S126. Chapter 9's wiring investigation found that checks read a separate empty ledger; its intended genuine/sham content comparison was not implemented. Use `S126_WITNESS_SOURCE_CORRECTION_2026-09-26.md` for those runs. Their result cannot decide whether an effective loop experiences anything, and the local positive memory results must remain visible alongside failures.

The earlier V3 conclusion that a possible subject “cannot be reduced to the record it keeps” could be mistaken for a settled rejection of functionalism. The rewrite leaves that metaphysical dispute available while making the operational distinction firm: a successful history-sensitive correction is evidence about control; its relationship to feeling requires a specified account and further tests.

## Translation and the remaining conclusion

- **Silent radio:** separates signal from the route that reveals it. The brain has no detachable little experience-broadcast; injury can affect several capacities required by the test.
- **Closed door:** unresponsiveness can conceal different dynamics. Later dream reports and intervention responses supply converging evidence.
- **Game character and rubber hand:** correspondence can alter ownership; knowing which avatar responds is distinct from felt bodily ownership.
- **Whirlpool:** processhood is compatible with reality. Fluid organization alone does not specify an experiencer.
- **Mary:** possessing the account and undergoing the encounter are different events. The interpretation of that distinction remains a philosophical contest.
- **Denied payment:** a constructed institutional scene. Functional accountability can proceed while the experience question is investigated; no new legal rule or actual denial is claimed.

The chapter eliminates silence as a universal absence test, fluent self-report as a universal presence test, a material label as an adequate substrate argument, and unresolved consciousness as a reason to suspend corrigible governance. The process account is kept as the author's strong positive direction and required to face its own rival predictions. The end hands to Chapter 13: the ledger can govern a person before we settle the inner life of every participant in the arrangement.
