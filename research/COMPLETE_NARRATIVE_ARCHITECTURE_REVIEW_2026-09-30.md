## Scene depth

The opening act moves quickly through a broad inheritance question, while financial cases linger on amounts, dates, documents and decisions. This is the clearest subject for developmental feedback. A further opening scene should earn its length through a learner's failed attempt and a correction that materially changes the next attempt. Repeating the map-room explanation would not supply that depth. No invented dialogue or interior states have been added to historical cases.

The strongest scenes already have a cost and a consequential next act: the KB Toys payout letter, Green Mountain's check, Hamilton's excluded explanation, the misdirected machine check, and the unfinished proposal. Their different legal and experimental outcomes must remain visible. A commercial voice should let those events carry the conclusion before calling for another framework.

## Progression

The sequence is coherent. Each act changes the use of the inherited record, rather than merely displaying another example of memory. The legal-to-cognitive transition follows the reader who gives a document force. The cognition-to-machine transition follows the second witness. The machine-to-agency transition follows the authority to amend. No chapter relocation is warranted by this review.

Chapter 13 is the best place for an editor to test repetition and pacing. Its recap is doing a legitimate synthesis job; the nearby five-job explanation also revisits the same cases. Do not remove the formal argument or evidence merely to shorten it. Ask where the reader has already earned the inference and can proceed.
