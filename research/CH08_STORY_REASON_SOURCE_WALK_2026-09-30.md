The experiment separates plot ordering from reason strength. Immersion was self-reported; attitude was a distinct outcome. Narrative order and argument quality were manipulated, whereas depth of immersion was not randomly assigned. Therefore the chapter describes the result among immersed readers without claiming that randomly induced immersion caused a larger gain. Strong and weak reasons were defined by a separate pretest's ratings, rather than independent verification that the claims about food or policy were true. The chapter does not endorse the smoothie claim.

The explanatory addition gives the reader a failed inference to recognize: knowing a taxonomy of dangers does not establish that a particular person instantiates one. The experimental comparison gives the author a corresponding obligation: narrative force cannot substitute for the argument it carries.
