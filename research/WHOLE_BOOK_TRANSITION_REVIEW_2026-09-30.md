## Targeted changes

The prologue's 'second thing' was ambiguous after a paragraph containing both rock and whirlpool. It now names the whirlpool. Chapter 10's close repeated a whole-book history just before returning to its machine word; the recap becomes a short successor question. The experimental vocabulary results and source notes remain intact. Chapter 13's personal handoff now names participation and adjustable conditions, preparing the next chapter's argument without suggesting total control over one's history or circumstances.

These are editorial wording decisions. No empirical claim, numerical result, primary citation, source note or dated prediction is removed. The synthesis recap in Chapter 13 and the final historical return in the outlook retain their distinct jobs: assembling the argument and closing its arc.
