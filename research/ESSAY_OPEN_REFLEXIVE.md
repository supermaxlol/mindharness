# Open and Reflexive: Why the System Cannot Step Outside Itself

## 1. Closed vs. Open — No Loan, Nothing to Surf

A closed system exchanges nothing with its surroundings. Seal hot tea in a perfect thermos: it settles to one lukewarm temperature and stays there forever. No weather inside. No life. Equilibrium is not calm — it is dead. No differences left to do work with.

An open system exchanges energy, matter, and information across its boundary. You do. A cell does. A market does. You eat ~2,000 kcal a day (~100 watts), breathe oxygen in, dump heat and CO₂ out, and in between you think and move. Cut any exchange and you equilibrate in hours. That is death.

Earth is open. The Sun hits it at ~5,800 K; Earth must dump the same energy back to space but glows as a 255 K body. That gap is the gradient that powers everything.

A solar photon is short and crisp — ~0.5 μm, ~2.5 eV. An Earth photon is long and dilute — ~10 μm, ~0.12 eV. Divide: 2.5 / 0.12 ≈ 20. For every **1 photon in, Earth emits ~20 out**. Energy in equals energy out, but entropy — how spread out energy is — has multiplied ~20×. Pick a different albedo and you get 19 or 22; the ~20× is arithmetic from Wien's law, not a metaphor.

That 1→20 is a loan. One $20 bill in, twenty $1 coins out. Same value, far more pieces, more disorder. The Sun lends concentrated energy; the second law demands repayment in dilute form.

Any system that cycles — returning ready for the next act — must dump waste to close the loop. Carnot's cap (1824): η = 1 − T_cold/T_hot. For Sun-to-Earth that cap is ~95.6%; real systems do far worse: photosynthesis 1–2%, car engines 25–30%. The waste is not a flaw to fix but the price of going again. We encode it as an **energy floor of 0.15** — the harness variable `energy` never hits zero, because a system that spent everything could not restart.

The cosmic ledger is the same story large. After the Big Bang entropy was ~10⁸⁸. Today it is ~3×10¹⁰⁴, dominated by black holes — one billion-solar-mass hole carries ~10⁹⁵ via S ≈ 10⁷⁷(M/M☉)². Heat death would be ~10¹²². Both ends — hot uniform soup at the start, cold dilute soup at the end — are structureless. Everything interesting lives mid-fall, like milk swirling into tea for seconds: a pattern existing only while mixing. No gradient, no structure. A sealed dish goes still. A market with no volatility has no price to discover. A mind with no unmet need has nothing to attend to.

The boundary matters as much as the gradient. Skin, skull, context window, ledger — each decides what counts as inside vs. outside and what to bind to a source. Lose the boundary and you get leakage: an LLM with no provenance ledger claimed it generated text it was merely handed — 98% over-claiming on fabrications in our tests.

## 2. Reflexive — The World Contains the System

Now put that open system inside the world it acts on:

```
 [ SYSTEM ] ──acts──► [ WORLD ]
 │ contains the system
 ▼
 [ SYSTEM ]
```

An archer and a target is a normal system: you observe, it does not change because you looked. A reflexive system is different: you are inside what you act on, so every action rewrites the conditions for your next perception. The world you meet next already contains what you just did. No outside step exists.

Two channels run at once. **Action → World**: you act and the world changes — you buy and the price ticks up $0.30, you rehearse a worry and a synapse inserts receptors. **World → System**: you perceive, but the world you sample already includes your trace. The next input is consequence, not an independent sample.

A thermostat is barely reflexive; a mind, market, or reputation is deeply reflexive — act on it and it reconfigures to neutralize what you exploited. Because you cannot step outside, three breaks are built in.

**Break 1 — Models rot.** Knowledge gets used up. An edge earning 8% on $10M, scaled to $1B, arbitrages itself away — spreads collapse 30–60% within months as others copy it. A recommender at 82% accuracy shapes what users see; its distribution drifts 2–5% per week and accuracy falls to ~64% in a quarter unless retrained.

**Break 2 — Measurement corrupts.** When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure. A hospital judged on "seen within 4 hours" posts a perfect median of 3h 50m — because patients waited in ambulances outside, uncounted by a clock starting at the door. The metric improved; care got worse. The sensor is part of the system.

**Break 3 — Truth moves.** Some predictions make or unmake themselves by being believed. "The bank will fail" triggers $40B withdrawn in 48 hours — and the bank fails; false until it made itself true. "We have enough vaccine" raises gatherings 35% and cases double — a reassuring prediction that made itself false.

## 3. Self-Observation Is an Action — and Where Leverage Lies

Here the loop bites hardest because observer and observed share the same wiring.

A rehearsed thought is not neutral. High-frequency, emotionally charged repetition recruits CaMKII, inserts AMPA receptors, and triggers late-phase LTP. About 5–10 minutes of tight replay can tag a trace for capture; hours of repeated winning myelinates the path up to ~100× conduction speed, so the fast path wins next time. A worry practiced a thousand times is not a thousand worries. It is one worry amplified a thousand times by its own winning.

Self-observation does the same. Label yourself "an anxious person" and you install a filter — retrieval bias runs 20–40% — that recruits confirming evidence. Introspection writes on what it reads. Timing makes it unavoidable: specialists compete and the winner is decided ~150–250 ms before the narrator explains it. You feel the choice as authored, but the explanation arrives ~200 ms late — a press secretary, not a president.

Having the witness and being able to use it are different capacities. We handed a discriminating model an explicit, truthful record of what it had written and performance fell from 0.82 to 0.67 balanced accuracy. Forced checking on a working process degraded it. The number that matters is γ — changed divided by diagnosed — not whether the camera is on.

You cannot opt out. Not rehearsing a pattern is rehearsing the default. Every moment trains something.

Leverage is therefore uneven. In **the moment (~200 ms)** the vote has resolved; freedom is veto — not acting on the winner. Small and narrow. In **habit (hours to weeks)** — myelination, consolidation, reputation — whatever wins repetition wins structure; "choose" quietly becomes "built." In **identity (months to years)** the self-story predicts and arranges its own evidence; change the story and you change what gets noticed. You do not mainly choose actions. You choose what kind of system will be doing the choosing.

Three limits keep this honest: exhaustion removes options before they surface (fewer options, not harder choices); you can steer the ship but did not choose the ocean or wind; and sometimes the chooser is the broken part — when stress has degraded the prefrontal capacity that would veto, asking it to repair itself is why pharmacology exists.

Open explains why there is anything to do — 1 in, ~20 out, floor 0.15, complexity only mid-fall. Reflexive explains why doing changes what "doing" meant — two channels, three breaks, self-observation as action, ~200 ms delay, ~100× amplification. Together they describe a system that must surf a loan it can never fully keep, on a surface that reshapes under its weight, with a narrator arriving late and a ledger helping only if you can read it. There is no outside view. Any observer you deploy is made of you. That is the structure.

## 1. The Functional Model We Have Built
We propose a human as an embodied, continuously learning, predictive, self-monitoring system that must stay itself while changing while entropy rises: WORLD/ENV → PERCEPTION → INTERNAL MODEL (Memory + Prediction + Context) → VALUATION (reward/threat/goals) → ATTENTION → THOUGHT → SELF-MODEL → [Automatic → impulse] vs [Reflective → awareness of impulse] → DELIBERATION ("What do I endorse?") → INTENTION/CHOICE → ACTION → OUTCOME → LEARNING → Updated Memory/Habits/Values/Predictions/Self-model → LOOP

System is recursive: not merely models world, but models itself modeling world. Self = constructed control model integrating memories, bodily states, goals, relationships, predictions, future selves → narrative I am this kind of person. No-self ≠ nothing — processes exist, entity doesn't. Map still guides. Thoughts arise nonconsciously (NONCONSCIOUS → thought/urge → AWARENESS → feed/don't feed → elaboration or fade); impulse is event not instruction (I want to keep scrolling → I notice I want → Do I endorse? → No → act differently — basis of agency). Awareness makes information available; attention decides what receives processing; both distinct from consciousness and from memory.

Consciousness as control process: selected information becomes available for flexible control — attention, planning, comparison, future simulation, self-monitoring. Simple automatic stimulus → response vs flexible situation → represent alternatives → remember past → simulate future → compare → choose — huge advantage for novel situations.

Free will as compatibilist, functional: increasing ability to act according to endorsed values even when another system pulls elsewhere. Not escaping causality, but organism becoming cause: impulse → awareness → alternatives → values/consequences → deliberation → endorsed intention → action → feedback. Parachute example: automatic jumping = danger + conscious context parachute/training/controlled → different expected outcome → recontextualized prediction. Repeated choice → plasticity → stronger pattern → more automatic — conscious choice trains automatic system, second-order loop.

Remaining question H1: Is subjective experience simply what it is like for such integrated self-monitoring system to operate, or could system perform all functions — perceive, integrate, remember, plan, self-monitor, select, report, learn, modify — without experiencing anything (functional zombie)? User intuition: no; genuine awareness of own processing is experience; no inner observer required (homunculus rejected: no little observer, system models itself).
