## Primary literature checks

### Planning and monitoring

[Gollwitzer and Sheeran, 2006](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0065-2601%2806%2938002-1), *Implementation Intentions and Goal Achievement*, remains the cue-linked planning source checked for Chapter 14. Its 94-test synthesis reports d = 0.65. The exact tea cue and two-minute start are book design choices.

[Harkin et al., 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26479070/), *Does Monitoring Goal Progress Promote Goal Attainment?*, was checked again against the primary abstract. It synthesizes 138 studies, N = 19,951, with goal-attainment d = 0.40. Physical recording and reporting moderated the results. The chapter offers a private record; public disclosure is not required.

### Future, obstacle, response

[Kappes and Oettingen, 2011](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S002210311100031X), *Positive Fantasies About Idealized Futures Sap Energy*, was checked through the primary publisher's abstract and study preview. Four experiments compare idealized positive fantasy with other thinking conditions. The chapter uses the distinction to join an attractive future to its actual obstacle, without treating all hope as harmful.

[Wang, Wang, and Gai, 2021](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.565202/full), *A Meta-Analysis of the Effects of Mental Contrasting With Implementation Intentions on Goal Attainment*, was checked in the primary full text, including discussion and limitations. Its model combines 24 independent comparisons from 21 articles, N = 15,907, g = 0.336. Publication bias may reduce the estimate. The authors discuss flexibility when plans fail; the workbook therefore permits an amended cue or strategy.

### Avoidance and return

[Sirois and Pychyl, 2013](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/91793/1/Compass%20Paper%20revision%20FINAL.pdf), *Procrastination and the Priority of Short-Term Mood Regulation*, was checked in the authors' accepted manuscript, particularly its opening argument. It is a review account of immediate mood repair and costs passed to the future self. The proposal's displaced bill translates that account. It does not diagnose every delay.

[Lally et al., 2010](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/ejsp.674), *How Are Habits Formed?*, was checked in the primary abstract. Ninety-six volunteers attempted a chosen behavior in the same daily context for twelve weeks; 82 supplied sufficient data. The report finds variable automaticity trajectories and no material effect from one missed opportunity. Its 18–254-day range is fitted time to 95% of an asymptote, partly extrapolated beyond the 84-day observation period. The chapter does not turn that range into a deadline.

## Claims retained with the right units

The guide is forceful about its operational demand: preserve the attempt, consult its trace, and carry a justified amendment into the next situation. Its short timings are scheduling aids. The container is a representation for action; no assay here measures phosphatase recruitment, LTD, or limbic segregation during the exercise. The molecular evidence remains in Chapter 14 with its own experimental units.

The worked week tests six attractive shortcuts: knowing enough, removing one distraction, waiting for ease, keeping an unread diary, maintaining a faultless streak, and maximizing a completion ratio. Each encounters a particular failure on the page. None is dismissed merely because the editor lacks familiarity with the author's compressed terms.
