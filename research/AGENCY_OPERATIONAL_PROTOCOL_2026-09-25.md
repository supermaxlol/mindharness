## 1. Predictive recalibration loop

Declared Niyyah (value-aligned) → Interoceptive Pause (muraqaba, veto Hawa) → Micro-Promise (100% completion) → Nocturnal Replay (sleep consolidation) → Updated Identity (Nafs al-Mutma'innah) → loop.

$$\text{Self-Trust Score} = \frac{\text{Intentions Realized}}{\text{Intentions Declared}}$$

Big vague goals kept halfway log Intention ≠ Outcome and the predictor learns to expect failure (then generates sabotage to stop you trying). Fix: micro-promises (e.g. open the file, review 1 row, 120 seconds), non-negotiable, 100% completion. The predictor re-baselines from "we always quit" to "our word holds weight."

Muraqaba: feeling is interoceptive telemetry, not executive order. Deploy the ~200ms veto — acknowledge ("I feel the urge to check my phone") while the body stays ("...and I stay seated 120 seconds anyway").

## 3. Fully integrated loop + daily cycle
