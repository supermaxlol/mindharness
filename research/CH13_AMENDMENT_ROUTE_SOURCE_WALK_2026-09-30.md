## The split at the counter

Paragraphs 11–14: branch trading statement completion was necessary to roll into the next period; no facility within Horizon disputed figures; staff had to contact the Helpline and cover discrepancies with money or income deductions. Paragraph 21 accepts that challenged sums were treated as undisputed debts. This is a description of the general institutional procedure, not an invented account of one specific screen encountered by Hamilton.

Paragraphs 15–18: Fujitsu held ARQ audit records, could amend branch data without the operator’s knowledge, and kept PEAK and Known Error Log records. POL had a contractual right to information. The court knew of no PEAK/KEL disclosure in the forty-two prosecutions before it. The new prose does not say the entire ARQ trail was never disclosed in every case, or that every recorded keystroke proved a true shortage. Those are different records and propositions.

## The connected correction route

Paragraph 6: Fraser’s findings in group civil litigation involving about 580 sub-postmasters provided a factual basis for the criminal appeals. Paragraphs 52–54: CCRC referral conditions are explained; only two appellants had previously appealed; exceptional circumstances were accepted for these referrals. A referral is treated as an appeal and its reasons as grounds. The court then exercises its separate power to allow unsafe-conviction appeals. Civil fact finding, review referral and conviction reversal remain distinct acts.

The chapter draws the structural inference: collecting evidence, enabling a review and changing an outcome need authority and a working handoff. These provisions are reported as they operated in this 2021 case, rather than offered as present legal advice. The passage makes no additional compensation or later-case claim.

## Reader bridge and rival

The till’s acceptance button and complaint telephone number are a constructed image. They give the reader a familiar contrast between two operations with different effects. The strongest institutional reply—records and complaints already exist—meets the documented location of evidence, missing disclosure and required procedural route. Transparency removes an obstruction, but it does not automatically confer the power to quash a conviction.

The correction did happen. The chapter preserves the court’s thirty-nine allowed and three dismissed appeals and avoids a general rule that every objection must be upheld. No new legal, clinical or machine experiment was run.
