## Reader bridge and argument

The spare-key/three-locker scene is an invented teaching image. It distinguishes success on one candidate set from coverage of the distinctions needed in another. A success flag compresses an event; reconstructing whether it tested a consequential distinction requires the original request, alternatives, signal and choice. This audit requirement is the book’s operational inference, not a new measured intervention.

The strongest rival is that improved transmission certifies improved expressivity. The source distinguishes those measurements: ease of reproduction improves without corresponding communication improvement; unique signals decline. The new passage follows the actual selection criterion before asking separately whether its inherited convention distinguishes the relevant cases.
