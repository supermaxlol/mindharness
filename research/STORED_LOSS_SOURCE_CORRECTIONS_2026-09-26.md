## What survives

The central mechanism survives: credit risk can form before the final shortfall is known; accounting recognition, cash recovery, and public resolution happen on different dates; a rescue assigns contingent and realized costs to particular parties. A useful conceptual *S* is the gap between the best expected recovery and the value still carried by the relevant record. It is **not** directly observed by a credit-to-GDP gap or a charge-off series. The loss bearer must be established from transaction and resolution records.

## Remaining analysis work

If the book later needs a measured *S* series, define it at instrument or bank level from expected cash flows, carrying values, recoveries, and revisions. Do not back it out of BIS gaps, headline defaulted debt, or CPI/M2 co-movements. A fully reconciled FFIEC extraction would require matching call-report definitions, reporting populations, dates, and stock versus flow tags to FDIC Table III-A.
