## The controlling inference

**You are the maintenance of the loop.** A person participates in preparation, deliberation, learning, environmental change, and correction across time. The fact that those activities have causes does not erase their consequences. The chapter earns that philosophical inference through measured differences between tasks, biological interventions, and behavioral interventions. It keeps the supplied three scales—moment, habit, identity—as a useful organization, rather than presenting them as a universal measured hierarchy.

The same morning carries the argument. The verdict “I never finish difficult things” contributes to withdrawal and gains another apparent confirming instance. Later, the phone is elsewhere, the next task is specified, and an earlier prediction can be compared with the actual attempt. The final page asks whether an amendment enters the next action. ## Primary findings used

| Source | Finding carried into prose | Unit and scope retained |
|---|---|---|
| [Libet et al., 1983](https://doi.org/10.1093/brain/106.3.623) | Preparatory activity precedes reported intention in a self-initiated movement task. | The roughly 200 ms interval is reported intention **before movement**, not a universal narrator delay after action. Original sample: five subjects across repeated sessions. |
| [Johansson et al., 2005](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16210542/) | Some participants explain a substituted face choice without noticing the mismatch. | Choice blindness is a separate manipulation of introspective explanation. No universal rate of fabricated reasons is asserted. |
| [Schurger et al., 2012](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3479453/) | A stochastic accumulator accounts for the averaged buildup and generates a tested prediction. | A competing mechanism for the readiness potential; onset of the average is not automatically a committed decision. |
| [Maoz et al., 2019](https://elifesciences.org/articles/39787) | Readiness potentials differ between arbitrary and deliberate choices with matched display and motor response. | Eighteen analyzed participants; charitable donations were real but lottery-contingent. The study supplies a task comparison, rather than proof of uncaused will. |
| [Giese et al., 1998](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9452388/) | Blocking CaMKII Thr286 autophosphorylation disrupts tested LTP and spatial learning. | Mouse intervention, hippocampal CA1 and Morris water maze. This does not identify one permanent molecule carrying a lifelong memory. |
| [Frey and Morris, 1998](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9704995/) | Strong stimulation can stabilize earlier weak potentiation on a separate pathway; protein synthesis matters. | Rat hippocampal slices and a time-dependent tagging/capture comparison. No diary-induced synaptic capture was measured. |
| [Barco et al., 2002](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11893339/) | Altered CREB activity changes the threshold for persistent potentiation and informs capture. | Experimental expression in mouse CA1. The correct DOI is **10.1016/S0092-8674(02)00657-8**, in *Cell*. |
| [Harward et al., 2016](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature19766) | A stimulated spine releases BDNF and activates its own TrkB pathway, supporting structural and functional plasticity. | Cultured murine hippocampal slices, individual spines. This is a local mechanism, rather than a universal CREB→BDNF→actin assembly line. |
| [Purves et al., 2001](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK10921/) and [National Research Council, 1992](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK234245/) | Myelin greatly increases conduction speed; the NRC text explicitly describes a factor up to 100. | Fiber-level conduction. Purves gives about 0.5–10 m/s unmyelinated and up to 150 m/s myelinated; the chapter's 1 versus 100 comparison lies within these ranges. |
| [McKenzie et al., 2014](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6324726/) | Blocking new oligodendrocyte production impairs complex-wheel learning while sparing performance of a previously learned skill. | Mouse motor learning; acquisition versus retained performance. New myelination is distinct from the numerical conduction contrast. |
| [Lee and Wilson, 2002](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12495631/) | Ordered hippocampal sequences recur during post-experience slow-wave sleep at approximately twentyfold compression. | Temporal compression in rats, not a twentyfold human learning improvement. |
| [De Vivo et al., 2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5313037/) | Synaptic contact areas differ across sleep/wake conditions, with selective downscaling. | Mouse cortical ultrastructure. Largest synapses are relatively spared; the prose does not multiply every connection by the same constant. |
| [Gollwitzer and Sheeran, 2006](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0065-2601%2806%2938002-1) | If–then plans improve goal attainment on average. | Ninety-four independent tests; standardized effect *d* = .65. No 65% success-rate increase. |
| [Harkin et al., 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26479070/) | Progress-monitoring interventions improve attainment; physical recording moderates the benefit. | 138 experimental studies, 19,951 participants; standardized effect *d* = .40. No guarantee for one person or this exact routine. |

## Refinements that preserve the argument's strength

1. **Keep 200 ms; put it on the correct side of movement.** The archived +200 ms timeline conflates preparation, report, and later explanation. Libet and choice blindness now have separate roles. A fixed personal “executive veto window” is not inferred from the interval.
2. **Keep 100×; retain its physical unit.** Conduction and learning-dependent myelination both have evidence. They are two findings, not a measured hundredfold gain in a rehearsed thought. The McKenzie comparison provides the causal failed walk.
3. **Deliver the molecular detail.** CaMKII, tagging/capture, CREB, BDNF, myelin, replay, and scaling are translated through marking, stabilization, structure, conduction, and maintenance. CaMKII is not equated with the entire synaptic tag.
4. **Make agency consequential.** Deliberate choice, cue-linked plans, learning, and monitoring replace a merely verbal answer to fatalism. The definition of freedom remains the book's argued interpretation.
5. **Make the record amendable.** Retain an original dated prediction, append the result, and use a correction at the next encounter. Neither an uncorrectable verdict nor an immaculate completion ratio is the governing objective.
6. **Keep structural costs visible.** Some changes require care, negotiation, collective action, or altered institutional conditions. The financial argument still governs the personal coda: costs must not be assigned to someone who lacks authority over their source.
